# Phase 9 — Evidence quality

New full workflows carry `qualityPolicy: 1`. The decision confidence cap is derived from a deterministic evidence review at the saved DECIDE timestamp, and can never raise the model's rating. Older checkpoints retain the original policy and remain readable. No SQL migration is required: the policy marker and enriched evidence are stored in existing validated JSON snapshots.

The verifier can provide structured primary/secondary/community/unknown source profiles and supporting excerpts. Profiles must cover each supplied source exactly once when present. Every quoted excerpt must match its referenced source content after whitespace normalization; unmatched quotes are discarded, logged as source-ID audit markers and downgrade the claim to Unverified (or retain Conflicting). Foreign references still reject the output. Missing structured assessments remain explicitly unknown, and missing quotes prevent a stronger rating. Quotes demonstrate traceability to an excerpt, not proof that the claim is true.

The review considers claim verification, contradictions, excerpts, source kinds, publication/retrieval dates, publisher groups, unresolved unknowns, assumptions, critic concerns, research coverage and recommendation citations. Any unresolved limitation caps confidence at Low. Otherwise the ceiling is Medium. High is withheld because distinct domains and model judgments alone do not establish editorial independence or complete evidence. No percentages are invented.

Publisher grouping uses the Public Suffix List through `tldts`, including private suffixes. Subdomains of one publisher do not count separately; hosted tenants can have distinct domain groups, which still do not prove independent ownership. Known tracking parameters and fragments are removed for duplicate-page detection and Tavily result deduplication. Functional query parameters remain intact.

Only explicit ISO publication metadata returned by search is accepted. Missing publication dates stay unknown; dates are never inferred from snippets or URL paths. RetrievedAt remains retrieval time. Version-one review thresholds are one day for travel retrievals, thirty days for other retrievals, and 365 days for publication dates. A threshold means review is due, not that a fact necessarily became false. Future timestamps are flagged. Undated supporting evidence cannot justify Medium confidence.

The workspace shows current evidence quality, saved versus current recommendation confidence, source-kind explanations, publisher groups, quoted excerpts and conflicting claims. The current review accounts for elapsed time without rewriting historical recommendations or triggering paid research. Refresh status fetches saved data, not new sources. A new run covers at most three priority questions, merges duplicate pages across searches and states how many planned questions remain open. Older checkpoints without the question list remain readable as one-question runs.

Tests cover public/private suffixes, tracking duplicates, functional URLs, Medium ceilings, contradictions, missing quotes, publication uncertainty, stale/future dates, fabricated excerpts and historical checkpoints. Live tests additionally exercise the updated verifier through the complete authenticated workflow.

Verified on 2026-09-11: 212 offline tests passed. All 15 browser scenarios passed across the final regression runs, including three real analyses (one clarified run, one standard run and one retry). Live validation exposed non-verbatim model quotations; they now produce discarded-quote audit markers and downgraded claims. Strict schema/reference failures still stop the run and allow explicit retry. Lint, TypeScript and production build passed.
